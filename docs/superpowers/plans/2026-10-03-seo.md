# SEO for shidhartho.com — plan

_Date: 2026-10-03 · Status: proposed_

**Goal:** make `shidhartho.com` the top result for "Shidhartho Roy", give Google enough linked
identity data to build a knowledge panel, and make link previews and AI answer engines describe
the work correctly.

**What already exists:** `<Seo />` emits title, description, canonical, Open Graph, and Twitter
tags; JSON-LD has `Person` and `WebSite`; there is a sitemap, an AI-friendly `robots.txt`, and
`llms.txt`. The description is 158 characters (within Google's snippet length). So the work is
mostly *connecting* identity signals and fixing what the domain move left stale, not starting
over.

## Where the leverage is (in order)

1. **Getting indexed under the new domain.** Until Google has crawled `shidhartho.com` and
   accepted the move from `royshidhartho.github.io`, nothing else matters. This happens in Google
   Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, not in code.
2. **Links from authoritative pages.** For a name query, a link from a `cmu.edu` lab or department
   page, ORCID, and your Scholar/LinkedIn/ResearchGate profiles (done) outweighs any on-page tweak.
3. **Entity data.** A single `Person` with a stable `@id`, a `ProfilePage` wrapper (Google's
   documented type for profile pages), and your papers linked to that person by DOI. This is what
   knowledge panels and AI engines use to connect "Shidhartho Roy" to the work.
4. **Presentation.** A correct social card (the current one still says `royshidhartho.github.io`
   and uses the old blue accent), icons for search results and phones, and a real 404 page for old
   `/blog/` links.

### Not doing (and why)

- **Meta keywords:** ignored by Google and Bing; leaving the existing tag is harmless.
- **Wikidata/Wikipedia entries:** notability rules make these likely to be removed for a PhD
  student; a self-created entry can backfire.
- **Separate pages per paper:** would add thin pages with little unique content; the single page
  with linked DOIs is the stronger signal at this stage.

## How we'll know it worked

- Search Console shows `shidhartho.com/` as indexed (days to a few weeks after the move).
- A "Shidhartho Roy" search returns `shidhartho.com` first, with the favicon and the new title.
- Pasting the URL into LinkedIn or Slack shows the new red card with `shidhartho.com`.
- The Rich Results Test recognizes `ProfilePage` with no errors.
